You've probably seen us gather around in a meeting room like this once a week. You might have even been in the meeting your self. What actually happens in "Check-in Sessions"?

![[photo_2025-12-20 13.55.13 2.jpeg]]

Check-in sessions are one of the core systems that make up Efuye Gela. This is the central space where we take care of our most important pillars, **PEOPLE**! 

Wether someone is an Executive, Manager, Expert or Intern, attending check-in sessions is imperative to be kept in the loop and collaborate with one another. We reflect on our past and chart our future in the space that the check-in sessions give us.

There are 5 primary reasons that make Check-in sessions a very central system for EG. Those are:
## Recaps: 
This is where we reflect and recap our week. In every recap session, we attempt to answer the following questions
- What have we done? 
- How much of our target's have we progressed? 
- Anything interesting happen the past week?
- What are we planning to do for the next week?

## Experience sharing/ Workshops
The base of check-in sessions from it's beginning has been experience sharing/ workshops. 

Before check-in sessions were started, we'd meet every Saturday to attend a workshop that is centered around a theme or concept. Maturity models, team formation, Efuye Gela business models and much more were discussed on Saturdays. This provides a platform of learning for all members of EG and any guests that are interested to attend.

As it is now, Tuesdays and Saturdays are open for anyone and everyone to book a session and share their view points/ experiences to the team.

## Team Bonding
Forming and maintaining a high performing teams that are ever growing in new horizons needs a platform where the members of that team get to know one another. We need to know each other beyond titles and responsibilities, beyond tasks and reports, and most importantly, beyond the version we have of one another in our heads.

This is one of the places that give us the chance to practice one of our core values to one another. [[Empathy]]

![[photo_2025-12-20 13.54.57 2.jpeg]]

We can only obtain the capacity to empathize with one another by talking to each other and spending time together, how ever brief that time maybe. 

A key pattern we have seen after running check-in sessions for 1.5 years is that one of the key indications someone might be leaving or is misaligned with Efuye Gela is that person missing a significant number of check-in sessions, online or in person.

We get parts of our sense of belongingness, community and connection from our check-in sessions, making it a foundation of what makes us **Efuye Gela**

## [[Introvention]]
Introventions are a once per quarter activity we perform as a team in order to introspect individually as a team!

I know that sounds weird, so lets break it down.

[[Introspection]] is the action of reflecting back on what we have and have not done and extracting key insights/ lessons from our past that will inform our future actions.

[[Introvention]] is where we and other people in our team reflect on our past 3 months from the perspective of [[EG Values]]. These reflections are not meant to discourage, attack or point out the subject of the Introvention at that time for their actions. The core purpose is to supercharge our growth by letting our team members tell us about our blind spots or things we thought weren't that important.

For a full explanation and breakdown, please refer the full page on [[Introvention]].

## Announcements/ Team Rituals
Check-in sessions are very ideal for broadcasting/ announcing important updates that come from the Executive and/ or the management team. These include but are not limited to major strategy discussions/ announcements, 

Team Rituals, on the other hand, are activities that are performed in order to accomplish a certain objective but in a structured and ritualized way. Our foremost team ritual is [[New Member On-boardings]].
